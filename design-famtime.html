Famtime Food - Beans & Rice Flour

Food packagingBrand world

Famtime is about the table everyone gathers around - so the brand was built on warmth. Steaming akara, a bowl of pap, a kitchen full of light, wrapped around clean, trustworthy packs for its beans flour and rice flour. The photography sells the feeling; the pack design earns the trust.

What it's for

A food brand world plus retail packs for a beans-flour and rice-flour range. The packaging is meant to earn trust on the shelf, while the campaign photography is built for billboards, posters and social to sell the warmth of a shared family meal.

Tools & software
drawCorelDrawimageAdobe Photoshop

Artwork and dielines built in CorelDraw, with mock-ups and retouching in Photoshop.
