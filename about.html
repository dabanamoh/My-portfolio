The path behind the work

Different disciplines.
A shared way of thinking.

My background combines design with procurement and operations, and more recently product management. Those perspectives help me consider both how something looks and how it will work inside an organisation.

Across a website, a workflow or a physical space, I start with the same questions: who is this for, what are they trying to do, and what is getting in their way?

The foundation / Design

Thinking through making.

Interfaces, packaging, visual identities and spatial concepts taught me to work with hierarchy, constraints and the details of execution.

The context / Operations

Understanding how work happens.

Procurement and operations experience brings a practical view of business priorities, stakeholders and handoffs.

The focus / Product & AI

Connecting direction and delivery.

Research, strategy, prototyping and implementation come together in products that solve an understandable problem.

How I can contribute
