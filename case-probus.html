Overview

Run HR on a dozen spreadsheets and three chat apps, and something always slips through. Veridox is the calm alternative - one role-aware workspace where attendance, payroll, approvals and team safety finally live in the same place.

Owners, HR and managers share one Company workspace; employees get a focused app of their own. An AI co-pilot reads the day's raw activity and hands it back as a plain-language performance summary - then quietly watches in-app chat and email for safety risks - safe messages are never stored. I owned it end to end — from research and strategy through to a shipped and launched product, with every flow refined until a dense HR system felt genuinely light.

Features

Built around the work, not the org chart.

Dashboard

A workspace that briefs you

The owner lands on an AI-written summary - what's going well, what to improve - above live counters for tasks, approvals, clock-ins and recognitions.

People

The whole company, one directory

Search people by name, role or department; jump to records, departments and your team - with message and call one tap away on every card.

Approvals

Approval chains you design once

Requests route to the right people for sign-off - approving advances to the next step, rejecting stops the chain. No more email threads.

Time Tracking

Clock-ins that know where work happens

Employees clock in with one tap and see today's session, hours and timesheet in one view. Set the office on a map, and anyone clocking in beyond it is asked why - working from home, running late, a client visit.

AI Safety

A co-pilot that watches for risk

An LLM scans in-app chat and outgoing email for serious risks - fraud, harassment, burnout, information leakage - and surfaces scored alerts. Safe messages are never stored; flagged ones are kept for HR to review.

Problem · Process · Craft

Three years, one calm workspace.

African SMEs run people operations across spreadsheets, chat groups and memory - so things slip: a missed clock-in, an unpaid advance, an unanswered complaint. Veridox set out to put all of it in one role-aware place, and make a dense HR system feel genuinely light.

01

Research the problem

I started with research and a survey into how SMEs really run people operations day to day - and exactly where it breaks down.

User researchSurveyWorkflow mapping
02

Name it, architect it

The first cut was even called “Integrity Merit” - too cliché and hard to recall - so it became the memorable “Probus”, and today it ships as “Veridox”. Then I defined four role-based portals and nine modules, so each person sees only the tools they need.

NamingInformation architectureRoadmapping
03

Build & ship the MVP

I built and shipped the working product - dashboards, a payroll engine, approval chains - then layered in an AI co-pilot that narrates performance and an AI safety watch that never stores safe messages.

AI integrationPrompt engineeringRapid prototypingUI/UX
04

Two versions, now in beta

At least two full versions and nearly three years of iterating on real flows - tightening the experience, hardening data isolation and tuning the AI. It's now in beta testing.

The result

A serious HR platform that never feels heavy.
