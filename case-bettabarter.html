bettabarter.vercel.app/guest

Overview

BettaBarter turns decluttering into a game. It borrows the swipe-to-match model people already know and points it at trading real things.

Browse a deck of items near you, swipe right on what you want, and when two people like each other's things it's a match. A guest mode lets anyone try the swipe before signing up — and a layer of trust-and-safety design makes it safe to actually meet and swap. I led the product end to end, building the MVP with AI and refining each user flow with it.

Features

Designed to earn trust, then a trade.

Guest swipe

Try it before you sign up

Anyone can swipe a deck of real items with no account and no card — the fastest possible way to understand what the app is for.

List an item

Say what you have — and what you want

Add photos, video, category and condition, plus a "Looking For" field that powers smarter matches. The barter intent is built into the form.

Profile gate

Chats stay locked until you're real

A blocking step asks for age, location and phone before you can message — so every match is a verified, local, 18+ person, not a throwaway account.

Trust & safety

Private, but verified

Your real name and a government ID stay private and are used only for verification — quietly raising the bar for safe, in-person meetups.

Onboarding

Three steps, then you're in

A single friendly card teaches the whole model — swipe, match, swap — before handing people straight to the deck.

Experience highlights

System thinking, made playful.

swipe

A borrowed mental model

Swipe-to-match needs no explanation — people arrive already knowing how to use it.

lock_open

Mutual chat unlock

Both sides must opt in before a conversation opens — cutting spam and proving intent on each match.

verified_user

Trust designed in

18+ checks, KYC, ID upload and local matching make a stranger-to-stranger swap feel safe.

translate

Four languages

English, French, Portuguese and Arabic, translated in-app for mixed-language communities.

bolt

Real-time by default

Live chat and notifications keep momentum between a match and a meetup.

dark_mode

Warm, with a dark mode

A coral, friendly palette that keeps the focus on the items — light or dark.

The result

The hard part wasn't the swipe. It was trust.

Most of the design work went into the layers around the fun part — the gates, the verification, the mutual unlock — so two strangers feel safe enough to actually meet and trade. The result is a complete, multilingual platform you can try right now as a guest.
